Title: $680 Million Power Move: KEO World and Maha Capital Unite to Redefine the Economics of Business Credit
Date: 2025-10-06
Author: KEO World
Category: Company Updates
Image: https://www.keoworld.com/path/to/image.jpg
Image Caption: KEO World & Maha Capital deal announcement
Excerpt: KEO World is partnering with Maha Capital in a major $680 million transaction to combine its Workeo credit operations and scale globally.

KEO World ("KEO") has signed an agreement to sell its Mexican, Brazilian, Canadian, U.S. credit subsidiaries and the Workeo IPR to Maha Capital ("Maha") in exchange for 190,230,619 Maha shares and simultaneously Maha closed a capital raise of $35M from Strategic Institutional Investors, which brings the total net cash to $140M.

The transaction will combine KEO's credit operations with Maha's capital platform, creating a group capable of potentially generating $10bn in transactions with its current balance sheet.

KEO World Inc. launched Workeo (patent pending) in 2021, a cutting-edge B2B Buy Now Pay Later digital payments platform designed to assist medium to large enterprise businesses in digitizing their invoice payments and accessing instant credit options. In 2022, they introduced Workeo Travel to enhance efficiency for these businesses in managing their cross-border corporate expenses.
